# Red Team Debate — 2026-08-28

## Method deviation (stated, not hidden)

`red-team-workflow.md` Step 5.5 auto-rejects findings without `file:line`
citations **from the codebase**. This repo is empty (`git rev-parse` fails, no
source files). Spawning four cold reviewer subagents would produce findings that
the evidence filter rejects by construction, at full token cost.

Deviation: the four adversarial lenses were run against the **plan documents**,
which are real artifacts and are cited `file:line` below. Reviewers were not
spawned as subagents; the lenses were applied in-session, where full design
context already exists. Evidence quality is therefore plan-level, not
code-level — appropriate for a greenfield plan with no code to verify against.

**Findings:** 12 (9 accepted, 3 rejected) · 0 Critical, 5 High, 7 Medium

## High

### 1. Pricing coefficients are a sample of two competitors, presented as settled
**Lens:** Assumption Destroyer · **Location:** `plan.md:60` "Decisions already settled"; `phase-06-estimator-pricing.md`
**Flaw:** The price table was derived from exactly two French freelance sites
(Kōji, Enzo Gazzoli). `plan.md:60` files it under settled decisions. The
2304-combination test in Phase 6 proves the model is *internally consistent* —
it says nothing about whether 5 000 € is the right floor for a French business
application.
**Failure scenario:** Test suite green, model coherent, prices 40% under market.
Every quote leaves money on the table, and the passing test creates false
confidence that pricing was validated.
**Disposition:** **Accept**
**Fix:** Move pricing out of "settled" into an explicit assumption with a
review trigger: revisit after the first 3 quotes. Add to Phase 6 a note that
the test verifies consistency, not market fit.

### 2. English copy is unreviewed and treated as production-ready
**Lens:** Assumption Destroyer · **Location:** `phase-03-i18n-routing-content.md:20`
**Flaw:** "132 strings already drafted in both languages — reuse, do not
re-translate." Those strings were drafted in one pass, by me, unreviewed by any
native or professional. The EN page sells to English-speaking businesses.
**Failure scenario:** A UK prospect reads slightly-off English on a page whose
entire pitch is meticulousness. The medium contradicts the message.
**Disposition:** **Accept**
**Fix:** Add a proofreading step to Phase 3 todo before EN goes live. Budget
1h or a paid review.

### 3. Launch is blocked on an unassigned open question
**Lens:** Failure Mode Analyst · **Location:** `plan.md:82` Open Questions; `phase-07-legal-compliance-fr.md:69`
**Flaw:** Phase 7 is P1 and "blocks public launch", and cannot close until the
published-address decision is recorded. That decision sits in Open Questions
with no owner and no date.
**Failure scenario:** Everything is built, then launch stalls for weeks waiting
on a domiciliation contract nobody started.
**Disposition:** **Accept**
**Fix:** Pull the address decision to Phase 1 as a parallel non-code task, or
mark it explicitly as the project's critical path.

### 4. "Launch" is undefined when assets never arrive
**Lens:** Failure Mode Analyst · **Location:** `phase-05-media-scroll-recadrage.md:9` and `:66`; `plan.md:38` Goal 7 (P2)
**Flaw:** Goal 7 (real imagery) is P2, but Phase 5 calls missing assets "a
launch blocker for the work section only". Phase 8 success criteria never say
whether placeholders may ship.
**Failure scenario:** Page goes live with three hatched grey rectangles labelled
"Capture projet 01". For a client evaluating a designer, that reads as abandoned.
**Disposition:** **Accept**
**Fix:** Phase 8 gate: either real images, or the work-cards section is removed
from the launch build entirely. No placeholders in production.

### 5. No spam or rate-limit commitment on the only contact channel
**Lens:** Security Adversary · **Location:** `phase-06-estimator-pricing.md:55` and `:75`
**Flaw:** Phase 6 hands the configuration to "`mailto:` or a form service".
Rate limiting is conditional — "if a form service is used later". Phase 7
publishes `contact@` and, by legal obligation, full identity.
**Failure scenario:** Address harvested, inbox floods, a real lead is lost in it.
Single point of contact, single point of failure.
**Disposition:** **Accept**
**Fix:** Decide the contact mechanism in Phase 6, not later. If a form: server
validation, rate limit, honeypot, and name the processor in the privacy page.

## Medium

### 6. Repo is not a git repository; plan claims `branch: main`
**Lens:** Failure Mode Analyst · **Location:** `plan.md:7`; `phase-01-start.md` (no `git init` step)
**Flaw:** `git rev-parse --is-inside-work-tree` fails. Frontmatter asserts a
branch that does not exist. Phase 1 never initialises the repo.
**Disposition:** **Accept** · **Fix:** Add `git init` + first commit + remote to Phase 1 step 1.

### 7. Localised slugs are unnecessary complexity for a 6-page site
**Lens:** Scope & Complexity Critic · **Location:** `phase-03-i18n-routing-content.md:49`
**Flaw:** A slug map, reciprocity tests, and a route-mirroring switcher, so that
EN reads `/services` instead of `/prestations`. Two locales, few pages.
**Failure scenario:** Map drifts, one side 404s, hreflang cluster breaks — the
exact risk Phase 3 already lists.
**Disposition:** **Accept** · **Fix:** Same slug both locales for v1. Revisit if EN traffic justifies it.

### 8. Three font families on a monochrome page
**Lens:** Scope & Complexity Critic · **Location:** `phase-01-start.md:31`; `phase-08-qa-deploy.md:61`
**Flaw:** Phase 8 already admits the Lighthouse budget may fail on fonts and
proposes dropping to two — after Phase 2 has built a type scale on three.
**Disposition:** **Accept** · **Fix:** Decide in Phase 1. Archivo variable (width axis) covers
display + text; keep JetBrains Mono. Two families, decided before the scale is built.

### 9. Effort estimate is optimistic, Phase 3 especially
**Lens:** Assumption Destroyer · **Location:** `plan.md:6` (34-46h); `plan.md:46` (Phase 3 = 7h)
**Flaw:** 7h for typed dictionaries, locale routing, reciprocal hreflang,
localised slug map, route-preserving switcher and locale formatting.
**Disposition:** **Accept** · **Fix:** Phase 3 → 10-12h, or drop the slug map (see #7) and keep 7h.

### 10. Full CI stack may cost more than it returns at this size
**Lens:** Scope & Complexity Critic · **Location:** `phase-08-qa-deploy.md` Architecture
**Flaw:** Playwright + axe + Lighthouse CI + three grep gates for a solo
brochure site.
**Disposition:** **Accept (reduced)** · **Rationale:** The a11y class of bug this catches was
real and invisible by eye (2.71:1 contrast shipped in the prototype). Keep axe +
the grep gates; make Lighthouse a manual pre-launch check rather than a CI gate.

### 11. Estimator built before the site has any traffic
**Lens:** Scope & Complexity Critic · **Location:** `plan.md:47` (Phase 6, 5h)
**Flaw:** 5h plus tests on a configurator for a page with zero visitors. A
"from X €" line would validate the pricing question at a fraction of the cost.
**Disposition:** **Reject** · **Rationale:** The estimator is the deliberate differentiator against
both benchmark sites, and it doubles as proof of the exact skill being sold.
Cutting it removes the page's strongest argument. Scope stands.

### 12. Scroll recadrage assumes the 16% without support do not matter
**Lens:** Assumption Destroyer · **Location:** `phase-05-media-scroll-recadrage.md` Key Insights
**Flaw:** Firefox share among French B2B buyers is unmeasured.
**Disposition:** **Reject** · **Rationale:** Degradation is a static, correctly-cropped image.
No information or function is lost. Measuring the share would not change the build.
